About the Artist

Hello, I'm James Andrew.

Welcome to my digital art diary. Ako lang naman si James, ang isa sa pinaka-gwapo sa mundo, joke!! This website shows how grateful I am for this bunch of people around me.

I am blessed, thanks to God! Thank you for sending these people who help me achieve my dreams and become the best version of myself. This diary represents my meaningful journey as a college student.

I'm glad that I've experienced this. Na kahit mahirap ang buhay, nakapag-provide pa rin ang parents ko para makapag-college ako. Yes, mahirap maging estudyante. May times na nakakapagod, nakaka-stress, at nakakatamad. Pero kung pagod tayo, mas pagod ang mga nagpapaaral sa atin.

Hindi lang puro negative, kasi maraming times na masaya at nag-enjoy ako. Kaya kahit makapoy, magpatuloy lang!
